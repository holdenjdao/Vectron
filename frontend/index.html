The crew

Six Roles, One Assembly Line

Step 01

Commander

Reads the brief and plans the build.

Picks the closest blueprint, or designs a new system when none fits.

Step 02

Architect

Derives the system specification.

Subsystems, modules and topics, then dispatches the rest of the crew.

Step 03

Draftsman & Integrator

Draws the diagrams and wires the core.

Blueprint-style drawings, the runtime, the composition root and the ICD.

Step 04

Engineers × N

Fabricate every module in parallel.

Each module is built from the parts library and ships with its own tests.

Step 05

Inspector

Holds the quality gate.

Runs the checks and writes the inspection report before anything ships.

Step 06

Quartermaster

Packages the bundle.

Docs, manifest and hashes, ready to download whole or by module.
